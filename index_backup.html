Why this project

Estonia is among the most digitally advanced countries in Europe. Its research data management is not.

Fewer than ten people provide dedicated research data management support at the University of Tartu, for more than 2,200 academic staff — most of them doing it alongside another job. There is no institutional data management policy, no career path for data stewards, no formal curriculum, and no accepted way to measure what good data management is actually worth.

ELEVATE-DM addresses all five gaps at once, working with two of Europe's leading research data infrastructures. The Netherlands has made data stewardship a recognised university profession. Finland runs national data management services at scale. We are building the Estonian equivalent — and producing the evidence that lets institutions justify paying for it after the project ends.

•

Objectives

Strengthen RDM capacity at Tartu and across Estonia

Better skills, clearer structures, and coordination between the units that support researchers.

Make data management contribute to research excellence

Workflows that raise the quality and reusability of research data rather than adding paperwork.

Build professional capacity that lasts

Structured learning pathways, microcredentials, and recognised roles for data stewards.

Professionalise communities of practice

A working data management community spanning Estonian institutions and sectors.

Improve institutional support systems

Clear roles, connected services, and machine-actionable data management plans.

Generate evidence on the value of well-managed data

The research component: what does data management investment actually return?

Build lasting European partnerships

Transferable models other widening-country institutions can adopt directly.

Consortium

Three institutions, already connected through the ELIXIR network before the project began.

EE — ESTONIA

University of Tartu

Coordinator

Estonia's leading research university and host of the ELIXIR Estonia node since 2013. Operates the DataDOI repository and serves as the national OpenAIRE and RDA contact point.

NL — NETHERLANDS

Health-RI

Advanced partner

The Dutch national coordination point for health and life sciences data infrastructure, hosting ELIXIR-NL. Brings FAIR implementation, the data stewardship profession, and community governance.

FI — FINLAND

CSC — IT Center for Science

Advanced partner

Finland's national IT centre for research and host of ELIXIR Finland. Brings training delivery at scale, national RDM services, and machine-actionable data management planning.
